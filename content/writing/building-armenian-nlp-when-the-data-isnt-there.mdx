Most NLP tooling carries silent assumptions: Latin script, English-ish morphology, and a mountain of labeled data somewhere upstream. Armenian violates all three. It has its own 39-letter alphabet, rich inflectional morphology, and two written standards — Eastern (spoken in Armenia) and Western — that tools regularly conflate or ignore. The labeled corpora that do exist are small, uneven, and often built for one narrow task.

So I built a toolkit, and — just as deliberately — a README that reports its limitations next to its results.

## What I'd tell someone building for another low-resource language

- Spend the first budget on normalisation and evaluation data, not on models. A better model measured against a broken test set teaches you nothing.
